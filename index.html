How to play

Explore the records, solve three deterministic puzzles, and follow the evidence to the final message.

  • Choose an unlocked archive file from the left index.
  • Enter an answer and press Enter or select Submit.
  • Use progressive hints if you get stuck. Each hint is counted.
  • Your progress saves in this browser. Reset removes this game's saved progress.

Keyboard: Tab to navigate, Enter to activate, Escape to close this panel.
